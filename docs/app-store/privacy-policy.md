# Earned It Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Earned It is a shared chore app for families. It is free and contains no ads, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking.

## Information stored by Earned It

Earned It stores the information a family enters and creates in the app, including:

- family and member names;
- selected profile roles and avatars;
- chore titles, notes, schedules, assignments, and history;
- completion, excuse, streak, and weekly progress records;
- optional allowance amounts and currency choices;
- invitation, device, membership, and synchronization identifiers needed to connect the correct family member and device.

Earned It does not ask for an email address, phone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or advertising identifier.

## Local storage and iCloud

The app keeps a local journal on each device. A family can initially exist only on one device.

When family sharing is enabled, Earned It sends the family journal to Apple's CloudKit service. The data is kept in the family owner's private iCloud database and made available through Apple's shared database only to invited participants. Earned It uses CloudKit for app functionality, synchronization, invitation acceptance, membership recovery, and access management.

The developer does not operate a separate server that receives the family's chore data. Apple processes iCloud data under Apple's agreements and privacy policies.

## Family access

An approved parent chooses the profile and role associated with an invitation. Invited family participants can access the household data made available through the CloudKit share. A participant with Apple read/write share access has broader technical access to shared records than Earned It's parent and child interface permissions. Families should invite only people they trust.

## Camera

Camera access is optional and is used only to scan an Earned It invitation QR code. Earned It does not save or upload camera images. A person can enter an invitation code or link instead.

## Analytics, tracking, advertising, and third parties

Earned It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, third-party telemetry, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not use App Tracking Transparency or the advertising identifier, and it does not link activity with data from other companies for advertising or measurement.

Apple may independently collect App Store, operating-system, iCloud, diagnostic, or crash information according to the person's Apple settings and Apple's privacy policies. Apple may make diagnostic or aggregated App Store information available to developers. Earned It does not treat that platform activity as first-party behavioral tracking and does not use it to track people across apps or websites.

## Retention and deletion

Family history is retained so the app can preserve past assignments, progress, and allowance outcomes.

On a family that has never enabled sharing, a parent can choose **Delete All Local Data** in Settings. This removes that family's local data from the device and cannot be undone.

On a shared installation, a parent can choose **Disconnect This Device** after pending changes have synchronized. This removes the local family copy from that device but does not delete the owner's CloudKit data or copies held by other family devices. The family owner controls the CloudKit share and can revoke invited access. Removing the app from a device removes its local app data according to iOS behavior.

Earned It does not currently provide a developer-operated account or server-side deletion portal. The CloudKit family owner manages the shared family and its participants. Apple also provides controls for iCloud data through the person's Apple account and services.

## Children's privacy

Earned It is designed for use within a family. A parent creates child profiles and decides which people and devices receive invitations. The app does not use children's information for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

## Changes to this policy

If Earned It's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released.

## Contact

Privacy or support questions can be filed in the public [Earned It issue tracker](https://github.com/jlm429/earned-it/issues). Do not include family names, chore details, invitation links or codes, Apple account information, or other private data in a public issue.
