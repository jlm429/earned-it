# App Store release checklist

Updated October 10, 2026 against `origin/main` at `d88d9749ddbf087e75346e6fffd0e64f11d7af03`, version 1.0.4, source build 16. This is a submission checklist, not a claim of App Store availability or signed Production readiness. See [the final audit](../final-release-readiness.md) for evidence and proof limits. Release remains blocked by the receipt-lost-after-uninstall deletion gap until the separate [recovery fix](https://github.com/jlm429/earned-it/pull/44) is integrated and signed two-account/device parent/child validation passes; this documentation lane neither implements nor validates that fix.

## Remaining release blockers

- [ ] **Lifecycle access policy.** First complete signed former-participant Development tests of both family and exact claim-revocation authorities under Authenticated Read/Create and Creator Write with no World permissions. Prove access to authenticated terminal evidence after shared-zone access is lost. Only after that proof may an authorized human change Production roles to match [the authoritative contract](../family-lifecycle-authority.md), then verify them. The October 10 Production type, fields, and World Read report is human-reported, not independently verified evidence of current configuration. Do not redeploy the schema solely because an older report says the type was missing.
- [ ] **Signed household validation.** Validate the final candidate with two physical devices and separate iCloud accounts: parent/child invitations, cold/warm QR and link acceptance, exact profile binding, completion sync, offline retry, relaunch, account membership recovery, read-only/revoked access, and older-client write refusal after an occurrence-aware action. Record the build and OS versions with each result. Follow the existing [invitation](../production-invitation-package/report.md) and [membership](../production-membership-recovery/report.md) procedures.
- [ ] **Deletion and generation safety.** Integrate the separately implemented and locally tested [owner-reinstall recovery fix](https://github.com/jlm429/earned-it/pull/44), which is absent from audited main and the final release. With signed two-account/device parent/child tests, interrupt after removal of the exact original owner zone while authority remains `deleting`, uninstall/reinstall the creator app to lose the local receipt, and prove authenticated `deleted` publication before old membership release/local clearing, followed by child terminal-proof Reset App. Recovery must bind the exact original household, generation/attempt, creator and current account from surviving authoritative records, verify exact original owner-zone absence, preserve account isolation, and reject missing/active/unauthenticated authority, stale bindings and non-creators. After Development access-policy proof and any authorized human Production role correction, also perform interruption, retained-receipt Finish Deleting, relaunch, account-switch, exact revocation, and same-parent/same-children fresh-family checks in [the deletion procedure](../delete-all-earned-it-data-implementation-report.md). Use an approved disposable household. Never test by deleting a real family's records.
- [ ] **Final signed artifact.** Through the authorized release workflow, archive and validate the candidate with the current required SDK. Inspect its actual identity/version/build, Production iCloud environment, container and `InProcessOneTimeLinks`, embedded privacy manifest/report, app icon, camera purpose string, and iPhone/iPad support. Run `scripts/validate-release-device-family.sh` on the archive. Simulator and unsigned generic-iOS builds cannot close this item.
- [ ] **Artwork and screenshots.** Confirm commercial rights for the icon and any promotional artwork. Approve the icon's current pre-rounded tile treatment or supply an approved master. Capture accurate final-candidate screenshots with fictional data at Apple's required iPhone and iPad sizes. Do not submit `assets/earned-it-preview.png`. See [visual-assets.md](visual-assets.md).
- [ ] **Privacy, support, and review access.** Confirm the public [policy](https://github.com/jlm429/earned-it/blob/main/docs/app-store/privacy-policy.md) and [support](https://github.com/jlm429/earned-it/issues) URLs are the intended maintained destinations, enter them in App Store Connect, reconcile privacy answers with the signed archive report, and provide private review instructions/resources for sharing. The policy and in-app links already exist on `main`.
- [ ] **App Store Connect inputs and distribution.** Confirm the app record/bundle selection, updated age questionnaire and category, content rights, copyright, review contact, agreements, export compliance, territories/DSA status as applicable, price, chosen build, and release method. The draft price is Free; repository code cannot prove live pricing. Authorize TestFlight distribution and smoke-test the selected candidate before submission.
- [ ] **Integrate the final improvements.** Punt, notifications, and UI polish are separate work in progress at this audited base. After integration, validate the combined candidate, update the feature/privacy/review claims, and recapture affected screens. Do not list pending features as available or promise background push from alerts generated only when journal changes arrive.

## Final device and accessibility pass

On the integrated signed candidate, check launch/relaunch and background/foreground recovery, camera denial/manual entry, airplane-mode queued work and later convergence, longest chore/member text, largest Dynamic Type, dark mode, reduced motion, status contrast, 44-point controls, and physical-device VoiceOver reading/action order. Include iPad portrait and landscape. Existing Simulator/accessibility evidence is useful but does not prove this final combined UI or physical-device experience.

If notifications land, check explicit contextual opt-in, denial/revocation, device-local settings, privacy of lock-screen content, correct eligible-child targeting, duplicate suppression/relaunch, and the app's usable experience with alerts disabled. The prepared Info.plist `UIBackgroundModes` `remote-notification` code-only edit is accepted normal implementation in the separate notification lane, remains pending and absent from audited main, and needs no separate approval for that edit. Signing/APNs entitlements or provisioning changes, CloudKit schema promotion, private/shared subscriptions for receiving accounts, and signed two-account/device evidence remain human release prerequisites. Delivery is best effort, not guaranteed; local alerts after refresh do not prove remote/background delivery.
