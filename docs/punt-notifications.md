# Legacy turn approvals and family activity

Current screens use [Swap Turns, bonus awards, and manual reminders](family-polish.md). This document records the earlier one-way transfer protocol and historical verification. Transfer statistics are retired. Existing transfer facts and accounting remain readable.

## Legacy one-way transfer behavior

Earlier versions let the child whose turn it was request a one-way transfer from today's alternating chore row to another active child in that chore's saved rotation. Current chore rows offer [Swap Turns](family-polish.md#swap-turns) instead. Pending legacy requests remain actionable in Activity as Earlier turn request. The original child remains responsible while approval is pending; the recipient can accept or decline and the requester can cancel. Pending requests appear first in Activity and survive relaunch and offline use. Mark Read changes only this installation's unread indicator; it never dismisses a pending request or makes a decision.

An approved punt changes only that occurrence's required child and allowance/streak credit. It leaves chore identity, recurrence, participant order, and the original rotation slot intact. Scheduled future dates follow the original rotation. An As Needed completion consumes the original child's slot, even when the recipient completes it; withdrawal consumes no slot. Each same-day activation has its own identity and approval boundary. One approved punt is allowed per occurrence. Declined or cancelled requests can be retried while that occurrence remains unresolved. This does not permit arbitrary transfers or changes to future turns.

Only today's unresolved scheduled or activated As Needed alternating occurrence is eligible. Requests expire at the next household civil midnight. Completion, Not Needed, withdrawal, deletion, or a displaced revision prevents later approval. Ineligible children cannot receive a punt. The requester cannot accept their own request and parents cannot approve it for a child. Accepted ownership remains in that date's history after midnight; configuration edits cannot rewrite it.

Once an occurrence has been accounted for or disposed, reversing its completion or restoring its requirement does not reopen the punt boundary. The original approval facts stay auditable and any existing approved owner stays attached to that occurrence. A new As Needed activation gets a fresh boundary.

## Facts, permissions, and conflicts

`PuntRequest` references chore, exact revision, household civil day, optional stable As Needed occurrence ID, requester, and recipient. `PuntDecision` references the exact request fact and scope, deciding child, civil day, and accepted/declined/cancelled action. Immutable facts use the existing SwiftData journal and household private/shared CloudKit zone. No backend, database, record type, or record field is introduced.

`HouseholdStore` and `PermissionService` check selected invited profile, write access, current responsibility, eligibility, and pending state. Projection replays Lamport sequence and UUID order. Duplicate decisions cannot change decided requests. A stale request cannot acquire another occurrence. A completion preceding a delayed decision closes the approval boundary. Concurrent offline decisions resolve deterministically; an offline result can change after reconciliation. A former-owner completion arriving after an accepted transfer remains audit-only and provides no active credit. Source history is never deleted to resolve conflicts.

Punt facts use `HouseholdFact` format version 3 and require journal capability 3. Older clients fail safely rather than changing newer ownership. Versions 1 and 2 remain readable. A punt fact establishes the newer-fact boundary for legacy As Needed occurrences while preserving their original activation and rotation interpretation. The [current compatibility rules](family-polish.md#compatibility-and-security) cover newer family facts.

These are app-level permission checks. CloudKit read/write participants retain broader transport access; role restrictions are not server-enforced. Existing exact invitation claims, account membership, participant management, and account-generation validation remain unchanged.

## Communication coverage and privacy

Durable activity derives from source facts. Children receive parent chore assignments/edits and available/restored occurrences, including every eligible alternating participant when a parent makes a chore available. An inactive As Needed definition does not request work. Activity also covers Not Needed, withdrawal, As Needed turn skips, chore removal/archive, parent completion corrections, excuses, allowance changes, and existing profile access requests/approvals/removals. Child completion updates appear for parents. Punt recipients receive requests/cancellations; requesters receive accept/decline decisions. Native invitation sharing still delivers invitations because an unjoined recipient has no authorized journal yet.

The bell opens Activity with an unread count. Every activity entry is visible, with pending turn requests before the Notifications card. All source facts remain in the journal. Read and alert receipts stay local to the household and member; ordinary entries use fact identity, while [manual reminders](family-polish.md#manual-reminders) use a derived identity. No analytics, contacts access, messages service, or third-party SDK is added.

Activity's Notifications card has an Earned It alerts switch and separate preference and iOS permission states. The preference is local to the installation's device session, independent of shared household facts and profile-targeted receipts. Existing authorized installations preserve their previous alert behavior until a preference is explicitly saved. Switching off persists across relaunch and ordinary iCloud account changes, prevents future system alerts and clears pending/delivered alerts, including a delivery completing after the switch changes. Denial leaves the feed and decisions usable. Only an explicit enable action in the undetermined state requests permission. Denied authorization instead offers Open iOS Settings; foreground return rechecks authorization. Off, denied and not-yet-authorized arrivals do not replay when alerts are enabled later. Enabling also records the selected profile's existing backlog without marking it read. A confirmed local session reset clears the preference along with the rest of the local session.

One generic summary alert covers a batch of incoming activity, including corrections to earlier dates. Lock-screen content contains no family or child names, chore titles, allowance amounts, or approval details. A tap opens Activity after normal profile routing. Profile changes and blocked/unavailable family access clear system alerts. Scheduling checks current authorization, following [Apple's permission guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/asking-permission-to-use-notifications).

## Background delivery and release prerequisites

Enabled code prepares APNs registration, an idempotent `CKDatabaseSubscription` for existing `HouseholdFact` records in the owner's private or invitee's shared database, silent change hints, a validated household fetch, then permission-controlled local alerts. Database subscriptions support shared databases and may coalesce changes. Record-zone subscriptions cannot be saved in a shared database. See [Apple's database subscription documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/cloudkit/ckdatabasesubscription).

Configuration/Info.plist now enables `UIBackgroundModes = [remote-notification]` under the recorded release-owner approval. The built app checks that mode before registering APNs, receiving background hints, or preparing subscriptions. Read-only owner/child preflights and dedicated UI-test launches disable all three paths. No signing identity, entitlement, provisioning profile, container, or production schema is changed, and agent tests create no production subscriptions. Enabled code does not prove signed APNs support or independent invited-account device delivery. Durable activity remains available after launch, foreground, or manual sync.

Human release actions: inspect the final signed release for the correct `aps-environment` entitlement and APNs-capable provisioning, and approve any missing signing prerequisite through the release process. Existing iCloud configuration alone does not prove the final signature. No new record fields/types are needed. Verify subscription configuration with approved development accounts; any necessary production schema promotion remains a separate human-approved release action.

A 25-second watchdog bounds background completion. Apple may delay, throttle, coalesce, or drop pushes. Force quit and disabled Background App Refresh can prevent wake. Durable Activity still syncs on launch, foreground, or refresh. Approval never depends on push delivery. See [Apple's background update documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/pushing-background-updates-to-your-app).

The watchdog cancels its caller and completes the iOS callback once. See the [technical overview](technical-overview.md#work-ownership-and-recovery) for synchronization ownership, foreground update limits, and retry behavior, and the [stabilization report](prerelease-stabilization.md) for cancellation regression evidence.
